# AI Statement
No AI tools were used in the creation of the Glina Creative Micro System, and
I would kindly request that anybody contributing to the Micro System does not
use AI tools of any sort to generate source code. Automated contributions (e.g.
those submitted by AI "agents") will not be merged.

The Micro System is about humans creating and using technology to create genuine
connections with other humans, and humans learning the fundamental principles of
computer hardware and software. I do not believe that the use of generative AI
is compatible with these goals, nor do I believe that generative AI tools are,
for the most part, being created and used ethically at the present time.

Australian copyright law does not currently have a "fair use" exemption for AI
training, and the use of this source code for AI training is explicitly
forbidden by the license agreement. I do not believe it is fair to use the work
of authors to train AI systems for commercial gain without acknowledging and
compensating those authors. I remain hopeful that, in the future, fair data
markets will be created to allow for the acknowledgment and compensation of
authors whose works are used to train AI systems for profit.

Some good resources (note: inclusion does not indicate endorsement of the
authors' positions):

* [The AI Con - How to Fight Big Tech's Hype and Create the Future We Want](https://thecon.ai/)
* [Federal government rules out changing copyright law to give AI companies free rein](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-10-27/labor-rules-out-ai-training-copyright-exceptions/105935740)
* [To Gen or Not To Gen: The Ethical Use of Generative AI](https://blog.johanneslink.net/2025/11/04/to-gen-or-not-to-gen/)
* [Ed Zitron's Where's Your Ed At](https://www.wheresyoured.at/)
* [New tech sucks. Neo-Luddism, or did past vintages really taste better?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gp6LgZ_-aLE&t=5500s)

Please do not take this to mean that I am judging anyone for their own choices
or usage of any tool. I do wish to be inclusive of everyone, however I feel it
is important to be clear about my own position and, as the project creator and
maintainer, to explain my goals for creating this project and what my
expectations are for it.

The above are only my opinions, and not intended to be statements of fact.

Please make up your own mind. I welcome differences of opinion and good-faith
discussion and, if you disagree, feel free (within the license terms) to fork
the project and create your own!
